glance

A calm, minimal clock for school. Your classes, homework, weather and music on one screen.

Open glance
Your school day

Always know what's now and what's next

Put in your bell schedule once. glance follows it every day, flips A and B days by itself, skips days off, and handles late starts and special schedules.

Class countdown

The class you're in, a live countdown and progress bar, what's next, when you're out, and anything due today. It can also show as a ring.

Day timeline

Your whole day as one strip, with a line showing where you are in it.

Today

Today's classes with times and rooms. Switch it to "Everything" for classes, homework, reminders and plans in one list.

This week

Every day of the week with its A/B letter and classes, so you know what to pack.

Calendar

A month view that marks every A and B day and leaves days off blank.

Progress

How far through the class, the day, the week, the month and the school year you are. There's a days-left counter too.

A/B day

Just the letter, big. On weekends it shows the next school day's.

Homework and planning

Everything you need to do, right where you'll see it

Import assignments from Canvas, plan your evening, and double-click anything to change it.

Homework

Import from your Canvas calendar, see what's due and when, and check things off. Due-today items show up under your class countdown too.

Tonight

Block out your evening. The current block lights up, and it clears itself the next morning.

Checklists

As many as you want, with due dates and drag to reorder.

Every day

A checklist that resets each morning, for the stuff you do daily.

Countdowns

Days and hours until the things you're waiting for.

Reminders

One-time or repeating, even "B days only". They can pop up with a sound.

Alarms

School-day alarms, weekend alarms and a choice of gentle sounds.

Habits

Tap a box each day and keep your streak going.

Study timer

Focus sessions with short and long breaks. A plain timer and stopwatch too.

Music

Practice tools built in

A metronome, tuner and polyrhythm trainer that work right in the browser, plus Spotify controls for whatever you're listening to.

Metronome

Type any tempo up to 500, any time signature, tap tempo, accents, and vibration on phones.

Tuner

Uses your mic. Viola, violin, cello, bass, guitar or chromatic, with A set anywhere from 400 to 480.

Polyrhythms

Hear and see any two rhythms against each other, like 3 against 2 or 4 against 3.

Flashcards

Paste a list, click to flip, and mark what you know. Great for vocab.

Spotify

See what's playing and control it on any of your devices: play, pause, skip, drag to seek and change the volume. Needs Spotify Premium.

Make it yours

Every widget goes where you want it

  • Press E to edit. Drag widgets to move them, drag a corner to resize.
  • Every widget has its own settings: size, alignment, what it shows.
  • Presets to start from: Student, Focus, Planner, Music, Minimal and more.
  • Several layouts, like one for school and one for home. They can switch by themselves.
  • Share a layout with a link, or a bell schedule with a short code.

Fine-tune anything

12 or 24 hour, seconds or not, a digital or analog clock, a dozen fonts, bright or soft text, see-through widget backgrounds and a finer grid for exact placement.

Backgrounds that follow the sky

The sky changes with the time of day and the weather outside. Or go pure black, use your own photo, and add stars, snow, leaves or fireflies.

Everywhere

Your clock follows you

  • Free account that syncs everything between your computers and your phone. Changes from different devices merge instead of overwriting each other.
  • Works without one too. Everything stays in your browser unless you sign in.
  • Phones stack your widgets in one column.
  • Stays awake so it can sit on a spare screen all day.
  • Backups as a file or a code, any time.

Find anything

Press Ctrl K and type to find any widget or setting.
